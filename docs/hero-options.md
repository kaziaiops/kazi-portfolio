# Hero headline options

Constraint: outcome plus who it is for, at most 4 lines at 375px, no invented claims.
Every phrase below maps to something already on the site (services list, spec ads for
SaaS and DTC brands, "same face, same outfit, shot to shot", solo voice-to-cut workflow).

Subhead (all options): "Voice, shots and final cut by one person, from Dhaka, Bangladesh."

## A (applied, tentative)
**AI video ads for DTC and SaaS, same face every shot.**
- Outcome: a consistent on-screen character across a whole ad.
- Audience: DTC and SaaS brands (the six spec ads and the UGC campaigns are exactly these).
- Why: names the buyer and the product in the first six words, and the second half is the
  one thing that sets this work apart. Narrowest option, so strongest for the right visitor.

## B
**Short-form AI video for brands, with a cast that never drifts.**
- Outcome: a cast that stays consistent across shots and episodes.
- Audience: brands, without limiting to ads.
- Why: wider net (covers the microdrama and character-driven short-form work). Slightly
  more abstract: "never drifts" is insider language until the visitor sees the work.

## C
**From your script to final cut by one person: AI video for brands.**
- Outcome: one point of contact for the whole pipeline.
- Audience: brands.
- Why: sells the solo, end-to-end process rather than the look. Reads like a capability
  list, less specific than A, but the safest if the visitor does not care about character
  consistency.
